Debt Reduction Protocols

Compare structured methodologies for paying off credit facilities, educational financing, and personal loans:

Avalanche Protocol (Highest Interest First)

Directs excess capital to balances carrying highest APR to mathematically minimize cumulative interest paid.

Snowball Protocol (Smallest Balance First)

Focuses on quickly eliminating smallest balances to build psychological momentum and simplify obligations.

Educational planning tool. FinVantage does not guarantee debt elimination or specific legal outcomes.
